# Solarist Brand Strategy & Digital System — Volume 02

## 1. Brand foundation

### Purpose

Solarist exists to help established organisations obtain dependable engineering capacity and improve the way technical work is delivered.

### Mission

To provide precise, practical and commercially useful engineering services across solar photovoltaic, solar thermal, battery energy storage, BIM content and AI-enabled process automation.

### Vision

To become a trusted engineering partner for companies that need specialist technical delivery without building every capability internally.

### Brand promise

**Engineering expertise. Practical automation.**

Supporting statement:

**Engineering systems for energy, buildings and better workflows.**

### Positioning

Solarist is a premium engineering agency for established medium-sized organisations that require reliable outsourced technical capability. It combines renewable-energy engineering, manufacturer-focused Revit content and practical AI automation under one disciplined delivery model.

Solarist is not positioned as a residential installer, generic AI startup, SaaS product, marketing agency or freelancer portfolio.

## 2. Primary customers

Priority customer groups include:

- Commercial solar developers, EPCs and contractors
- Engineering consultancies and energy-service companies
- Battery and energy-equipment companies
- Product manufacturers needing specification-ready BIM content
- Architecture, MEP and BIM teams
- Established companies seeking process automation or AI deployment

These customers are generally mature enough to outsource specialist engineering work and value accuracy, communication, schedule reliability and documentation quality over the lowest possible price.

### Typical buying triggers

- Internal engineering capacity is constrained
- A project requires specialist code or documentation knowledge
- A manufacturer needs production-quality Revit families
- Existing SolidWorks, STEP, SAT or DWG content must be converted for BIM use
- Repetitive internal work is slowing delivery
- Spreadsheet, document or data processes need controlled automation
- A company wants to introduce AI without compromising human review and accountability

## 3. Competitive difference

Solarist's advantage is the combination of engineering experience, practical automation capability and an understanding of how technical deliverables are actually reviewed, permitted, coordinated and used.

Founder-led proof may include:

- 8+ years of total engineering experience

- Lead Engineer responsibility on 39 projects
- Experience across 20+ California cities
- 7.1 MW of photovoltaic capacity designed
- 10,875 photovoltaic modules specified
- 800 kWh of BESS capacity designed

## 5. Messaging system

### Voice

Solarist sounds like engineers who care about design.

The voice is:

- Plain-spoken, not plain
- Calm and specific
- Technically credible
- Commercially aware
- Warm in one deliberate place
- Confident without exaggeration

Avoid words such as revolutionary, disruptive, cutting-edge, game-changing, world-class and best-in-class unless independently substantiated.

### Preferred message pattern

1. State the engineering or operational problem.
2. Explain the exact work Solarist performs.
3. Name the resulting deliverable.
4. Provide evidence or constraints.
5. Offer a clear next action.

### Core homepage proposition

**Engineering systems for energy, buildings and better workflows.**

Supporting copy:

Solarist provides commercial solar, thermal and BESS design; manufacturer-ready Revit content; and practical AI automation for established companies that need dependable external engineering capacity.

Primary CTA: **Discuss a project**

Secondary CTA: **Explore services**

## 6. Visual identity

### Core colours

- Ink: `#11100F`
- Ink 700: `#3C3834`
- Ink 500: `#6B635D`
- Paper: `#EBE3D6`
- Canvas: `#F7F4EF`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Line: `#D5CCC0`
- Sun display: `#D85A1A`
- Deep Sun text: `#A8400D`
- Ember decorative: `#ED7A3F`
- Success: `#2F6B4F`
- Warning: `#8A5A00`
- Error: `#B42318`
- Information: `#1F5F8B`

Use semantic tokens from the production files. Do not scatter raw colour values through the codebase.

Sun is a display and graphic colour. Deep Sun is the accessible accent for ordinary-sized text and suitable action backgrounds. Ember is decorative only.

### Typography

- Canela Deck: selected display headlines, high-value statements and editorial moments
- Inter: body copy, UI, navigation, forms, buttons and most headings
- JetBrains Mono: metrics, engineering values, codes, labels and metadata

Canela should create distinction without turning the website into a fashion or publishing site. Use approximately 10% Canela, 80% Inter and 10% JetBrains Mono.

### Logo system

Use only the production SVG files in this directory.

- Primary horizontal logo: ordinary brand identification
- Full 16-ray symbol: normal and large square contexts
- Micro symbol: favicon and very small digital contexts

Do not stretch, tilt, recolour, duplicate, animate or place the mark on visually noisy backgrounds.

## 7. Digital design system

### Layout

- Mobile-first responsive design
- 12-column desktop grid
- Maximum content width: 1280 px
- Flexible gutter based on the production tokens
- Comfortable line length, generally 55–75 characters for body copy
- Generous whitespace, but not at the cost of information clarity

### Components

At minimum, define and test:

- Header and responsive navigation
- Primary, secondary and text buttons
- Service cards
- Metric cards
- Case-study cards
- Contact and enquiry forms
- Tables and engineering-data displays
- Tabs and accordions
- Breadcrumbs
- Alerts and validation messages
- Loading, empty and error states
- Footer and legal navigation

Every interactive component requires default, hover, focus, pressed, disabled and error states where applicable.

### Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA.

- 4.5:1 minimum contrast for normal text
- 3:1 for large text and essential non-text graphics
- Visible keyboard focus
- Complete keyboard navigation
- Semantic HTML and heading hierarchy
- Proper labels, descriptions and error messages for forms
- Reduced-motion support
- Useful alternative text
- Information must not depend on colour alone
- Minimum practical touch target of 44 px
- Avoid small Sun-orange text on white or Paper

### Motion

Motion should explain state or hierarchy, not decorate the interface. Use subtle transitions, avoid perpetual movement and fully honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 8. Imagery and technical visual language

Do not use confidential client photographs, drawings or plans without written permission.

Approved placeholder categories include:

- Commercial rooftop photovoltaic systems
- Solar canopies and carefully framed energy infrastructure
- BESS equipment and controlled industrial environments
- Revit models, family-editor views and clean technical geometry
- Engineering drawings and diagrams with confidential details removed
- Workflow maps, document automation and structured data interfaces
- Engineers working with drawings, models or equipment in credible environments

Avoid generic hard-hat stock imagery, residential-rooftop clichés, leaves over solar panels, glowing AI brains, excessive blue holograms and oversaturated orange grading.

### Revit imagery

Show the product as a useful digital asset:

- Clean orthographic or isometric views
- Parameter panels and type structures
- Connector logic where relevant
- Before-and-after complexity reduction
- Manufacturer product detail without exposing confidential files

### AI automation imagery

Prefer systems diagrams and real workflow evidence:

- Inputs, validation, processing, human review and outputs
- Document queues and extraction interfaces
- Before-and-after process maps
- Time saved, error reduction and review controls

Do not visualise AI as magic. Show the controlled workflow.

## 9. Data visualisation

Charts and technical graphics must prioritise comprehension.

- Ink and neutral tones carry most information
- Sun highlights one important series or decision
- Deep Sun is used for accessible labels
- Use direct labels where possible
- Include units, assumptions, date ranges and data sources
- Do not rely on colour alone
- Avoid unnecessary 3D effects, gradients and decorative chart elements

## 10. Website information architecture

Recommended primary structure:

- Home
- Services
  - Solar PV
  - Solar Thermal
  - BESS
  - Revit & BIM
  - AI Automation
- Experience or Work
- Engineering Tools
- About
- Contact

Top-level navigation may group services, but every major service should have an indexable destination with useful, original content.

### Homepage sequence

1. Clear proposition and call to action
2. Service architecture
3. Founder experience and proof
4. Capability details for solar, BESS, Revit and automation
5. Selected non-confidential examples
6. Working process
7. Engineering tools
8. Founder or about module
9. Final contact call to action

### Founder module

Use a professional founder image when supplied. A restrained hover or focus overlay may reveal the name, role and concise credential statement. The same information must remain accessible on touch devices and to keyboard users; do not make hover the only way to access it.

## 11. Case studies

Use a consistent structure:

- Context
- Client category, anonymised when necessary
- Engineering challenge
- Constraints and codes
- Scope delivered
- Key decisions
- Deliverables
- Result
- Founder or team role

Never reveal client-confidential drawings, names, addresses, system details or commercial information without authorisation.

## 12. Governance

- Production tokens and SVGs are the code source of truth.
- Volume 01 governs the original identity principles.
- Volume 02 governs strategy, messaging, accessibility and digital implementation.
- Changes to canonical colours, logo geometry, type roles or core positioning require explicit approval.
- Keep the brand directory versioned in Git.
- Do not commit licensed font files unless distribution rights have been confirmed.
- Record image source and licence information.
- Review accessibility, responsive behaviour and build integrity before deployment.
